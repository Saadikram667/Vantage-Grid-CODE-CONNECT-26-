Algorithmic Architecture

How Vantage Grid Works

The triage decision engine evaluates seven physical, demographic, and logistical constraints to rank rescue priority in real time.

1

Survival Clock (Tc) & Self-Protective Extension

The base survival clock calculates the minimum safe duration a household can endure before an unmitigated hazard breaches safe limits. It evaluates rising flood depth relative to terrain and floor height, medical dependency under power outage degradation, thermal limits, and water/food reserves. Action steps confirmed by residents on the Check-in page physically inject verified mitigation bonus minutes (B) directly into the base clock:

Tc = max(5, min(T_flood, T_med, T_therm, T_sup, 2880)) + B
2

Uncertainty Dampening (k) & Cellular Grid Failure

Self-reported and verified households operate with high confidence (k = 1.0). Unknown or unregistered residences receive a shorter clock to pull them forward in the queue for early welfare checks (k = 0.5). If cellular network outages exceed 80%, the system assumes a total telecommunications collapse across the zone, automatically degrading k to 0.5 and applying an uncontactable penalty to ensure silent households are not left behind:

Tc_eff = Tc * (0.6 + 0.4 * k) [where k = 1.0, 0.8, or 0.5]
3

Severity Assessment (S) & Probability of Need (P)

Severity scales from 2 to 5 based on medical equipment dependence (oxygen/life support = 5, age 75+ = 4, chronic conditions = 3, healthy adults = 2), with an additional 0.5 applied to isolated individuals living alone. The probability of emergency need (P) increases when telemetry or check-in goes silent (+0.15), and collapses when marked safe (-0.40).

P = clamp(0.4 + 0.5 * (Confidence/100) + P_silent - P_safe, 0.02, 1.0)
4

Priority Index Score

Queue position is ranked from highest to lowest index score. The priority index directly balances physiological vulnerability against time urgency:

Priority = (P * S * 60) / max(Tc_eff, 10)
5

Fleet Dispatch & Passenger Capacities

Rescue assets are constrained by vehicle type, transit speed, water clearance limits, and strict passenger capacities (Boats: 6 people, Trucks: 15 people, Helo: 2 people). If picking up a household exceeds a unit's passenger capacity, the engine forces an offload detour to the nearest safe relief shelter (e.g., Civil Hospital, Expo Centre) before dispatching to the household, realistically expanding arrival ETA:

ETA = T_free + T_detour_shelter + (Distance / V_unit * 60) + T_pickup
6

The Rescue Gap Index

A rescue gap occurs whenever a household's effective survival clock expires before the fastest compatible rescue crew can reach their coordinates. Negative values indicate catastrophic delay:

Gap = Tc_eff - ETA

Households in a rescue gap are highlighted in alert coral on the live queue and designated with warning beacons on the crisis map.

7

Meteorological Telemetry Ingestion

Live simulation feeds ingest atmospheric conditions (temperature, wind velocity, and ambient moisture) directly from Open-Meteo API sensors covering the Karachi coastal basin. The regional crisis index calibrates automatically to real-world storm intensity, while command staff maintain manual override capability for simulated scenario rehearsals.
