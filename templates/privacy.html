Data Privacy Protocol

How Vantage Grid handles and secures your sensitive data during emergencies.

1. Data Collection & Purpose

Vantage Grid collects specific household and medical data solely for the purpose of emergency triage and rescue routing. This includes:

  • Geographical location (address, floor level, and neighborhood).
  • Demographics (number of occupants, ages over 75, individuals living alone).
  • Critical medical needs (reliance on oxygen, life support, or mobility constraints).
  • Contact information (emergency phone numbers).

This data allows the command center to calculate accurate survival clocks and prioritize dispatch units to households in immediate danger.

2. Data Anonymity & Security

To protect civilian identities, full names, exact addresses, and phone numbers are strictly firewalled. They are only visible to authenticated operators on the secure Dispatch Dashboard. They are never rendered on public maps or endpoints.

During the Check-In process, users are identified exclusively by their system-generated Household ID. The platform does not display a public registry of registered households.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

Vantage Grid is an ephemeral crisis tool. All civilian registration data, medical markers, and check-in statuses are automatically purged from the regional databases 72 hours after the emergency declaration is lifted by the Regional Emergency Management Authority (REMA). Data is never sold, shared with third parties, or retained for non-emergency analytics.
