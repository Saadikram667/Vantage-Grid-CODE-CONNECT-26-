About Vantage Grid

Vantage Grid is a dynamic triage and route optimization system developed for the Regional Emergency Management Authority (REMA) to orchestrate flood rescue and support and help those in a rescue gap.

The Challenge of Urban Floods

During rapid onset floods in dense urban areas like Karachi, conventional emergency dispatches fail to scale. Rescue requests flood in simultaneously, overwhelm existing response units, and leave commanders to guess where resources are needed most. Crucially, without a real-time predictive picture, units are often sent to stable, multi-story buildings while people with limited mobility or reliant on oxygen are trapped on lower ground.

Traditional map tools show where the water is, but not who is most vulnerable, how long they can wait, or where our crews would arrive too late.

Our Methodology: Real-time Resource Decay

Vantage Grid introduces a triage system built on survival clocks rather than queues. The moment a household registers and checks in, our algorithm evaluates and aggregates specific risk factor decay curves to construct their active survival duration: structural flood breach estimates, critical power outage effects on medical machinery, and isolation survival limits.

These clocks are constantly calibrated by real-time sensor updates and meteorological telemetry. Rather than dispatching on a first-come, first-served basis, Vantage Grid continually reroutes boats, amphibious trucks, and aerial assets to the most urgent time-to-decay gaps across the region.

Our goal is to identify "rescue gaps" early—homes where the fastest compatible rescue unit still won't arrive before their clock expires—giving command staff the foresight to stage assets early and issue directed evacuation alerts.
